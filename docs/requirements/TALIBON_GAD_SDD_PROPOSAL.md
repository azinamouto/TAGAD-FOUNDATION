PROJECT PROPOSAL TALIBON GAD-SDD SYSTEM Talibon Gender and Development Database and Sex-Disaggregated Data System I. PROJECT TITLE Establishment of the Talibon GAD-SDD System: A Web-Based Gender and Development Database and Sex-Disaggregated Data Management, Monitoring, Analytics, and Reporting System II. RATIONALE The Local Government Unit of Talibon recognizes the importance of evidence-based, inclusive, and gender-responsive planning and budgeting. In the implementation of Gender and Development (GAD) programs, the availability of accurate, updated, and sex-disaggregated data is essential in identifying gender issues, determining priority interventions, monitoring program effectiveness, and preparing required GAD reports. At present, data from various LGU offices and barangays are often fragmented, manually recorded, and not consistently disaggregated by sex, age, barangay, sector, and type of service or program. This limits the ability of the LGU to conduct gender analysis, generate timely reports, and develop responsive Programs, Activities, and Projects (PAPs). The Talibon GAD-SDD System is proposed as a centralized, web-based platform that will systematically gather, store, update, manage, analyze, and report gender statistics and sex-disaggregated data for the Municipality of Talibon. The system shall support the GAD Focal Point System (GFPS), LGU offices, and barangays in planning, programming, budgeting, monitoring, evaluation, and policy formulation. The proposed system is aligned with the following laws, policies, and guidelines: ● Republic Act No. 9710, otherwise known as the Magna Carta of Women; ● PCW-DILG-DBM-NEDA Joint Circular No. 2012-01; ● Harmonized Gender and Development Guidelines (HGDG); ● Gender Mainstreaming Evaluation Framework (GMEF); ● Data Privacy Act of 2012; and ● Other relevant GAD and local governance policies. Through the establishment of the Talibon GAD-SDD System, the LGU shall strengthen its capacity to develop gender-responsive programs and services that address the needs of women, men, girls, boys, and other sectors in the municipality. III. OBJECTIVES General Objective To establish a centralized web-based Gender and Development Database and Sex-Disaggregated Data System that will support GAD planning, budgeting, monitoring, reporting, evaluation, and evidence-based decision-making in the Municipality of Talibon. Specific Objectives 1. To develop a centralized data encoding and management system for sex-disaggregated data from LGU offices and barangays; 2. To generate real-time gender statistics, analytics, dashboards, and reports; 3. To support the preparation of the GAD Plan and Budget (GPB) and GAD Accomplishment Report (GAR); 4. To improve gender analysis and identification of gender-responsive Programs, Activities, and Projects; 5. To establish a secure and user-friendly digital platform for GAD-related data storage and retrieval; 6. To strengthen data-driven and gender-responsive governance in the Municipality of Talibon. IV. PROJECT DESCRIPTION The Talibon GAD-SDD System is a web-based application designed to capture, manage, monitor, analyze, and generate reports on gender statistics and sex-disaggregated data within the Municipality of Talibon. The system shall serve as the official digital platform of LGU Talibon for GAD-related data management. It shall enable authorized users from LGU offices and barangays to encode, update, retrieve, analyze, and generate reports on sex-disaggregated data and GAD-related programs and services. The system shall include data categories such as sex, age, barangay, sector, office, program/service availed, participation in trainings and consultations, access to social services, VAWC/Women and Children Protection Desk-related data, livelihood participation, health-related data, education, agriculture, fisheries, disaster risk reduction, employment, and other sectoral indicators relevant to GAD planning and development. The system shall also provide dashboards, analytics, automated reports, and export features to assist the GAD Focal Point System, MPDC, department heads, and other authorized users in preparing plans, reports, and policy recommendations. V. PROJECT COMPONENTS / SYSTEM MODULES 1. User Management Module The system shall provide role-based access for authorized users, such as: ● System Administrator; ● GAD Focal Person; ● Department Encoder; ● Barangay Encoder; ● Viewer; and ● Report Generator. This module shall ensure data security, proper user accountability, and controlled access to sensitive information. 2. Data Encoding Module This module shall allow authorized personnel to encode sex-disaggregated data based on the following categories: ● Sex: male, female, and other applicable gender categories; ● Age group; ● Barangay; ● Sector; ● Office or department; ● Program, activity, project, or service availed; ● Type of beneficiary; ● Date and location of activity; ● Other relevant GAD indicators. 3. GAD Program and Service Monitoring Module This module shall track GAD-related services, programs, and beneficiaries, including: ● Trainings and capacity development programs; ● Livelihood and employment-related programs; ● Health and social welfare services; ● Women and Children Protection Desk / VAWC Desk-related services; ● Participation in consultations and decision-making activities; ● Access to gender-responsive benefits, policies, and programs. 4. Dashboard and Analytics Module The system shall generate real-time visual summaries and analytics, such as: ● Male and female statistics; ● Barangay-level data; ● Sectoral breakdown; ● Age group distribution; ● Program participation by sex; ● Beneficiary profiles; ● Trends and comparative analysis; ● Gender gaps and priority concerns. 5. Report Generation Module The system shall generate automated reports to support: ● GAD Plan and Budget preparation; ● GAD Accomplishment Report preparation; ● Sectoral reports; ● Barangay-level GAD reports; ● Monitoring and evaluation reports; ● Data needed for policy formulation and planning. Reports may be exported to Excel, PDF, and other usable formats. 6. Data Security and Privacy Module The system shall include measures to protect personal and sensitive information, such as: ● User access control; ● Secure login credentials; ● Data validation; ● Backup and recovery; ● Confidentiality protocols; ● Compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. VI. TARGET BENEFICIARIES The primary beneficiaries of the project are: ● LGU Talibon Departments and Offices; ● GAD Focal Point System; ● Municipal Planning and Development Office; ● Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office; ● Municipal Health Office; ● Human Resource Management Office; ● Barangay Local Government Units; ● Sectoral groups and community organizations; ● Women, men, children, youth, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, solo parents, farmers, fisherfolk, workers, and other sectors in Talibon. Ultimately, the project shall benefit the people of Talibon through improved planning, better service delivery, and more gender-responsive governance. VII. IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY The implementation of the Talibon GAD-SDD System shall be undertaken in phases: Phase 1: System Planning, Design, and Requirements Gathering ● Conduct consultation with the GFPS, MPDC, departments, and selected barangays; ● Identify required data fields, indicators, forms, and reports; ● Prepare the system design and workflow. Phase 2: System Development ● Develop the web-based platform; ● Create database structure, user accounts, forms, dashboards, and reporting templates; ● Ensure data security and privacy features. Phase 3: Testing and Initial Deployment ● Conduct pilot testing with selected LGU offices and barangays; ● Validate system functionality, accuracy, and usability; ● Refine system based on user feedback. Phase 4: Training and Capacity Building ● Train system administrators, department encoders, barangay encoders, and report generators; ● Provide user guides and templates; ● Orient users on data privacy and proper data management. Phase 5: Full Implementation and Monitoring ● Implement the system across LGU offices and barangays; ● Conduct regular updating and validation of data; ● Monitor usage, report generation, and data quality; ● Provide technical support and system enhancement when necessary. EXPECTED OUTPUTS The project is expected to produce the following outputs: 1. Functional Talibon GAD-SDD Web-Based System; 2. Centralized gender statistics and sex-disaggregated database; 3. User accounts for LGU offices and barangays; 4. Standardized data encoding forms and templates; 5. Real-time GAD dashboard and analytics; 6. Automated reports for GPB, GAR, monitoring, and planning; 7. Trained LGU and barangay users; 8. Improved system for storing, updating, and retrieving GAD-related data. X. EXPECTED OUTCOMES The project is expected to contribute to the following outcomes: 1. Improved GAD planning, budgeting, monitoring, and reporting; 2. Strengthened gender-responsive governance in LGU Talibon; 3. Enhanced evidence-based decision-making; 4. Better identification of gender issues, gaps, and priority interventions; 5. Improved preparation of GPB and GAR reports; 6. Increased efficiency in data collection, analysis, and report generation; 7. More responsive programs and services for women, men, girls, boys, and other sectors in the municipality. XI. IMPLEMENTING OFFICE Lead Office: Municipal Planning and Development Office / GAD Focal Point System Support Offices: All LGU Departments and Offices, Barangay Local Government Units, and concerned sectoral offices System Users: GFPS members, department encoders, barangay encoders, planning staff, and authorized report generators XII. SUSTAINABILITY MECHANISM To ensure the sustainability of the Talibon GAD-SDD System, the LGU shall: 1. Designate system administrators and data encoders from concerned offices and barangays; 2. Conduct regular data updating and validation; 3. Include system maintenance and enhancement in future GAD-related plans and budgets; 4. Establish data privacy and security protocols; 5. Integrate the system outputs in the preparation of GPB, GAR, local development plans, sectoral plans, and policy recommendations; 6. Monitor system usage and continuously improve the platform based on user needs. XIII. CONCLUSION The establishment of the Talibon GAD-SDD System is a strategic initiative that will strengthen the Municipality of Talibon’s commitment to gender-responsive, inclusive, and data-driven governance. By developing a centralized and web-based system for sex-disaggregated data management, monitoring, analytics, and reporting, LGU Talibon will be better equipped to identify gender issues, design responsive programs, monitor results, and prepare timely and accurate GAD reports. The project shall support the LGU’s continuing effort to deliver more inclusive, equitable, and responsive services for all Talibongnons. &#x20;
