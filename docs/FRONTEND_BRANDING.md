# Talibon branding

The shared logo and decorative municipal watermark use the municipal hall mark
served as the site icon by the official Municipality of Talibon website.
Retrieved and visually inspected on 2026-09-29.

Source page: https://talibon.gov.ph/
Asset: https://talibon.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/cropped-cropped_circle_image-270x270.png

This is the municipal hall mark, not a claim that it is the legally adopted seal.
The pre-existing seal files remain available but are not used as verified current branding.
The hand-drawn seal recreation and TG placeholder have been replaced.
