## Questions for the first consultation

Confirm the six personas and data scopes; approve the field/indicator dictionary and service counting rules; identify the custodian of sensitive-service statistics; supply report templates and geographic/source data; designate pilot, maintenance and review owners. Capture D01–D13 answers as reviewable artifacts. No external messages or invitations were sent by this audit.

## Release decisions versus engineering choices

Engineering can reuse the stack, consolidate validators, preserve IDs, enforce known boundaries, and prepare additive migrations once authorized. LGU/DPO must supply institutional authority, collection/release policy, source definitions and acceptance. A time delay is not approval. No optional CBMS, feedback, AI, external-submission or case-management enhancement is included automatically.
